Filing Instructions

Before submitting your application, review the mandatory compliance configurations below:

1. Registration Prerequisites

  • Identity Verification: You must be 18 years or older with a valid government-issued ID.
  • Business Documentation: Business registration is preferred but not mandatory for the Micro-Business Grant.
  • File Formats: Supporting documents must be submitted as PDF or JPG. Each file cannot exceed 10MB.

2. Review Timelines

Once a tracking chain is logged, our evaluation panels audit compliance strings. Applications transition through these stages:

  • Awaiting Audit: Your application is in the queue and awaiting review.
  • Approved & Committed: Your grant has passed review. Funds will be disbursed within 7–21 business days.

3. Use of Funds

Grants may be used for equipment, inventory, marketing, training, technology, or any legitimate business expense. Grants may not be used for illegal activities, political campaigns, or personal luxury goods.

4. Impact Reporting

All grant recipients agree to report on the impact of the funds after 6 months. This is a condition of the grant and helps us improve the program.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this really free?

Yes. We will NEVER ask for payment, gift cards, OTP, or crypto to release your grant. If anyone asks, report them immediately.

How long does review take?

Standard review takes 15–20 business days. Priority review (for low-income countries) takes 10 business days. Emergency Recovery Grants are fast-tracked in 5–10 business days.

Can I apply more than once?

You can apply once every 6 months per email address.

What if I don't have a business registered?

You can still apply. We fund ideas and early-stage businesses too. Just be honest about your stage.

How do I get paid?

We disburse via Wise, Payoneer, direct bank transfer, or USDC stablecoin — your choice. No cash, no Western Union.
